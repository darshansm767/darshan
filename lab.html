Introduction

Laboratories play a vital role in a school's educational system, providing students with hands-on learning experiences that complement theoretical knowledge.

Importance of Laboratories

Hands-on Learning

Students apply theoretical concepts practically, enhancing comprehension.

Skill Development

Improves analytical, observational, and problem-solving skills.

Safe Experimentation

Controlled environment for conducting experiments safely.

Types of Laboratories

Physics Lab

Equipped with instruments like pendulums, circuits, and lenses.

Chemistry Lab

Contains chemicals, glassware, and safety equipment.

Biology Lab

Features microscopes, slides, and anatomical models.

Safety Measures

Protective Gear

Mandatory use of safety goggles, gloves, and lab coats.

Emergency Protocols

Regular drills and accessible safety equipment.
